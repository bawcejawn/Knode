How staking works
  • At least 1 VHP in a game makes you a staked player there for the week. Stake in one game, several or all of them.
  • A new stake counts from next week (Monday 00:00 UTC). Each game's weekly rewards are split by how much VHP is staked in it.
  • Your share is your stake divided by the game's total. Inside a game, the top 30% of staked players with at least 1 point share its rewards (more for a higher place and a bigger stake). Free players play for the 2 + 1 VHP free prize.
  • Withdraw any time, any amount, straight back to your wallet. Withdrawing part keeps you in this week at the lower stake.
  • Move stake from one game to another without leaving the vault; it counts in the new game from next week. You pay the Mana for a move yourself; staking, withdrawing and claiming can be covered by the Mana sponsor.
  • Rewards show after each week is finalized. Claim them one by one, or up to 10 at once.
  • Staking is optional and rewards aren't guaranteed. Terms & risks
