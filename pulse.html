What these mean

Block time: Koinos aims for a block every 3 seconds. Producers are picked in proportion to their VHP, so a longer average means fewer producers were online to fill the slots.

Producers to sign half: the fewest producers that together signed more than half of all blocks. The higher this number, the more spread out block production is.

VHP supply: KOIN burned into VHP to produce blocks. VHP slowly turns back into KOIN as it produces.
