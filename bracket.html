Producer Bracket

Sixteen Koinos block producers, one knockout bracket a week. Pick every winner before Monday; each day one round is played on real blocks. Matchups are handicapped by VHP, so a small producer and a big one have the same chance. A staked game: stake VHP to share the weekly rewards.

Leaderboard

Ranked by points; ties go to a right champion, then more right picks; players still tied share the place. The week's rewards go to staked players (1 VHP or more in the Bracket) by the standard KnodeGames rules: the top 30% with at least 1 point win.

How it works

The field. On Saturday at 12:00 UTC the bracket is seeded from every producer's exact VHP averaged over the day before (about 28,800 blocks), read from the chain on two nodes: the producers expected to sign at least 10 blocks a race, biggest VHP first. With 16 to 31 of them the top 16 play; with 12 to 15 the top seeds get a bye; with 8 to 11 it is an 8-producer bracket (Monday to Wednesday); from 32 it is a 32-producer bracket (Monday to Friday); under 8 there is no bracket that week.

Your pick. Fill in every winner, from the first round to the champion, between Saturday 12:00 and Sunday 23:55 UTC. The whole bracket goes to the chain as one number in one transaction; you can change it until the lock.

A matchup. Each round is played on one UTC day (Monday, Tuesday, …). A producer's index is the blocks it signed that day ÷ the blocks it was expected to sign. "Expected" uses its day VHP: the most VHP it held at any block of that day, rebuilt exactly from the chain's own VHP records. Koinos picks each block's signer in proportion to VHP, so a producer that keeps its VHP and signs its fair share scores about 100% whatever its size. In a matchup that comes down to blocks signed per day VHP. The higher index goes through; an exact tie goes to the lower seed.

Nothing to gain by moving VHP. Moving VHP away for part of a day only lowers a producer's real chances, since its day VHP stays the most it held; VHP added during a day counts for the whole day. What wins is staying online and signing.

Points. A right winner scores 1 point in the first round, then 2, 4, 8 (and 16 in a 32-bracket), plus 2 for the right champion. Byes score nothing.

Fair checks. Every hour of every round is recorded from irreversible blocks that match on two independent Koinos nodes, and the scoring code is public: this page and the recorder run the same file. A week is final 12 hours after its last round.
