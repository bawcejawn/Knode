Producer Race

Every Koinos block producer is a horse. Each block it signs moves it forward, and smaller producers get a handicap so every horse has the same chance. Four 6-hour races a day, starting at 00:00, 06:00, 12:00 and 18:00 UTC. Free, just for fun.

Picks: coming next

Soon: connect a wallet and pick one horse per race, up to 24 hours ahead. Picks lock when the race starts and are free. A weekly leaderboard follows the Knode Games week (Monday 00:00 UTC).

My picks

Free: one horse per race, for the next 4 races. You can change a pick until its race starts; then the chain locks it. Up to 5 picks a day (changes count). The Mana is paid by Knode Games' Mana sponsor.

Leaderboard

A pick on the winner is a win (3 points). A pick on the horse that finishes 2nd or 3rd is a show (1 point). Ranked by points; ties go to more wins, then more picks placed. Wallets still tied share the place. Weeks are Knode Games weeks (Monday 00:00 UTC). The Staked board is for wallets with VHP staked in the Producer Race that week; they share the Race's weekly reward bucket. Everyone else plays on the Free play board, where 1st and 2nd win the weekly free-play prize (2 VHP and 1 VHP). Both start once the Knode Games vault is live; until then every player is on Free play.

      How the handicap works

      Same speed. Koinos picks who signs each block in proportion to VHP, so a producer with a third of the leader's VHP signs about a third as many blocks. It moves 3 steps per block, which gives every horse the same expected speed. The biggest producer always moves exactly 1 step per block.

      Same chance. A small producer's few blocks come in lucky and unlucky bursts, which would let the smallest horses win most races. A luck trim evens that out:

      steps per block = (biggest VHP ÷ your VHP) × (1 − c ÷ √k) ÷ (1 − c ÷ √k of the biggest)

      Here k is how many blocks a producer is expected to sign in a 6-hour race (luck in k blocks is about √k). c is re-tuned on every card by thousands of simulated races (the same ones for everyone) to the value where each horse's chance of winning is as equal as it gets.

      Six cards per race. The handicap is set from VHP at the start and updated every hour. Each card applies to the blocks signed in its hour, so moving VHP around during a race buys at most an hour. Cards are recorded publicly by the race recorder.

      The finish line is the 6-hour mark. You see the real live order all the way to the end: the track is drawn so the leader gets close to the line, and only the winner crosses it when the 6 hours are up. The winner is the horse with the most steps at the 6-hour mark.
