Your Koinos @name

Swap your long wallet address for a name like @bluefin. It's yours on Koinos, so it shows in other Koinos apps too, and on every Knode Games leaderboard, result and producer page, always next to your short address.

1Your wallet

Connect your wallet to start.

2Pick your @name

@

3 to 32 characters: lowercase letters, numbers, dots and dashes, starting with a letter. Free: creating it only uses a little of your wallet's Mana, which refills by itself.

Good to know

  • Your @name is created on the Koinos Nicknames contract from your own wallet. Nobody else can change it.
  • Block producers that run a pool show their pool's name automatically.
  • Knode Games shows a new @name within the hour. Names that imitate Knode Games or Koinos (Knode, Koinos Foundation, official…) are not shown here, and the owner can hide an offensive one: the short address shows instead.
