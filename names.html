Play Knode Games

Name your wallet

Swap the long address for a name, for players and producers alike. It shows on the Knode Games leaderboards, results, Pulse and Producer lookup, always next to your short address, so nobody can pass for you. The name is free: you sign a short message with your wallet, and nothing is sent to the chain. (A Koinos @name, at the bottom of the page, is different: it's created on chain from your wallet.)

Already have a Koinos nickname (an @name from the Koinos Nicknames contract)? It shows here automatically until you choose a Knode Games name.

1Your wallet

A player: the wallet you play with. It needs at least 1 VHP in it or staked in Knode Games. A producer: the wallet that holds the VHP your node produces for (not the node's block-signing key).

Signing with another tool? Enter the address instead

2The name

3 to 24 characters: letters A–Z, digits, spaces and . - _ '. It can't look like another wallet's name or Koinos nickname, or be a reserved one (Knode, Koinos, official…). One change every 7 days.

3Sign

You sign this exact message. No transaction, no Mana, nothing moves.

Signed with another tool? Paste the signature

Sign the message above with that address's key (Koinos message signing: sha256 of the text, 65-byte recoverable signature, base64url). Paste it here.

Or get a Koinos @name

A Koinos nickname lives on the Koinos chain itself (the Nicknames contract), so it shows in other Koinos apps too, and here whenever you have no Knode Games name. Lowercase letters, digits, dots and dashes. Unlike the name above it is a real transaction from your own wallet: you pay the Mana (Kondor may hold up to 10 KOIN of Mana for it; Mana refills by itself, nothing is spent from your KOIN). The rules

  • Who can take a name: a wallet with at least 1 VHP in it or staked in Knode Games, or a block producer (on a Producer Race card or signing blocks in the last 30 days). The signature must come from that address.
  • Koinos nicknames (@names) show automatically for wallets without a Knode Games name. A Knode Games name always wins.
  • A claim is dated and works for 7 days; each new claim must be newer than the last one. One change every 7 days.
  • No two names that look the same (ignoring case, spaces and lookalikes like 0/O, 1/l/I, 5/S), including other wallets' Koinos nicknames.
  • Reserved: Knode, Knode Games, Koinos, Koinos Foundation, admin, official, support. The Knode Games nodes keep their names.
  • The owner can hide a name or nickname (the short address shows again). Every page always shows the short address next to a name.
