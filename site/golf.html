      How it works
      

        
The course. Every live Knode Games game is a par-4 hole. A new game joins the course the Monday after it goes live. There is nothing to pick in Block Golf: you just play the games.

        
Your strokes on a hole come from your place on that game's leaderboard for the week (Monday to Monday, UTC):

        

          

            
Your week in that gameScoreStrokes

            
          

        

        
Who you're placed against. The players that game ranks (they hold 1 VHP or have a Knode stake) and who scored on it that week; wallets that scored nothing don't count. If you played but scored nothing, it's a double bogey. Players on the same score share the middle of their places. A hole with fewer than 5 such players that week is skipped for everyone.

        
Practice weeks. Until Block Golf pays prizes, every player who scored counts (ranked or not) and 3 are enough for a hole, so early weeks still get scorecards.

        
The mulligan. Your worst played hole is dropped each week. A lost ball (a game you didn't play) can't be dropped.

        
The week. Lowest score wins. Points (what the vault uses) are 7 for each counted hole minus your strokes. Ties: more points, then fewer lost balls, then more holes under par, then your best hole.

        
Prizes. Block Golf is a staked game: stake at least 1 VHP in it once the Knode Games vault is live, and the top 30% share its weekly bucket. Until then, weeks are practice.

        
Fair checks. Block Golf only reads each game's published, final results. If a game's results aren't final 60 hours after the week, that hole is skipped for everyone, never guessed. The page and the recorder run the same scoring file.
