    How it works

    A weekly game about the Koinos chain. Your stake keeps producing blocks for the pool the whole time.

    1. Play free, or stake VHP

      Anyone can answer and climb the leaderboard for free. To share in the rewards, stake at least 1 VHP (whole numbers only), or KOIN, which is burned into VHP. A stake counts from the next week. You can withdraw any amount at any time, down to the last decimal, but withdrawing during a week cancels that week's answers (taking back only a top-up that hasn't started counting yet doesn't).

    2. Answer daily questions

      About 20 questions a week: new ones every Monday to Saturday at 12:00 UTC, and answers close at 00:00 UTC, 12 hours later, before the blocks they ask about are produced. Every correct answer is one point. Sunday is results day.

    3. The week closes

      Weeks run Monday 00:00 to Monday 00:00 UTC. After 12 hours for any corrections, the week closes, and on Tuesday at 12:00 UTC the winners are final. The pool's block rewards for the week are split: 93% to the winners, 5% creator fee, 2% kept by the owner. Players with the most points who together hold 30% of the stake win, and ties at the line win too. You need at least 2 counted answers.

    4. Collect your reward

      Your share is the pot × your points × your stake ÷ the same total over all winners, paid in VHP. Unclaimed rewards wait for you, and keep producing for the pool meanwhile.

    Who decides the right answer?

    The blocks do. After a question's window ends and the blocks are final, the answer is computed from them and published with a hash of the exact blocks used. If a producer in a question goes offline, the question is voided and doesn't count for anyone.

    Does it cost anything to play?

    No fees. Koinos uses Mana, which refills on its own. If your Mana is low, tick Kondor's free Mana option when you confirm.

    Can anyone take my stake?

    No. The contract only sends your stake back to you. The owner can't move players' stakes or rewards, and code changes must be announced 48 hours ahead so anyone can withdraw first.
