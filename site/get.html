Get KOIN and VHP

To share in the weekly rewards you need at least 1 VHP staked in Knode Games. Here is the whole way there, from nothing. You can also play free without any of this.

  1. 1

    Get a Koinos wallet

    KOIN Vault on your phone, or Kondor in Chrome on a computer. Write your recovery words on paper and keep them safe.

    KOIN VaultKondor
  2. 2

    Buy vKOIN

    vKOIN is KOIN on another blockchain. Buy it on Ethereum or Solana, whichever you already use. Always check the address below matches before you buy.

    On Ethereum
    0xa50ad3a559a10f384a5bb2e27516f63e0b937b1a
    Buy on Uniswap
    On Solana
    8AUxdPqYU4FBy5rZDhMJxTniPs7gtEfdHjP3UKM71m6G
    Buy on Jupiter
  3. 3

    Bridge it to Koinos

    Vortex moves your vKOIN to Koinos, where it arrives as KOIN in your Koinos wallet.

    Open Vortex bridge
  4. 4

    Turn KOIN into VHP

    Burn KOIN into VHP, 1 for 1. Keep a little KOIN: it gives your wallet the Mana it needs to send transactions. Or skip this step: Knode Games can take KOIN and burn it for you when you stake.

    Burn KOIN into VHP
  5. 5

    Stake and play

    Stake 1 VHP or more in Knode Games, then answer each day's questions. Your stake stays yours: take it back any time.

    Stake in Knode Games
  6. 6

    Want KOIN back? Swap your VHP

    VHP doesn't turn back into KOIN by itself. When you want KOIN again, swap your VHP on Knode Games Swap: one step in your wallet, small 3% fee. KoinDX, a Koinos exchange, also trades VHP for KOIN.

    Swap on Knode Games SwapKoinDX

Knode Games will never message you first or ask for your recovery words. Use only the links on this page, and check every address before you buy or bridge.
