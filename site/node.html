How the numbers work: each block goes to a producer at random, in proportion to its VHP among the producers that are running. "Expected" uses this address's current share of the active producing VHP; "actual pace" extrapolates what it really produced in the window. Short windows swing a lot, so trust a day or more. KOIN per block is the network's recent average block reward (KOIN minted to the producer minus the VHP its block used). The page keeps the blocks it has read in this browser, so the next visit only reads new blocks.
