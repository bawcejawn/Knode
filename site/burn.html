Burn KOIN into VHP

For your own wallet, e.g. the one your node produces for. Block rewards arrive as KOIN; burning them back into VHP (1:1) keeps your producing power growing. Your wallet signs (Kondor on a computer, KOIN Vault on a phone); this page never sees a key.

Every transaction costs a little Mana, and Mana comes from KOIN. Keep a few KOIN so the wallet can still send transactions (for a node, 5–10 is plenty).

Max = KOIN minus what you keep, and never more than your current Mana (Koinos only lets KOIN move up to the Mana you have right now).
