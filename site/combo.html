Combo Card

Stack 2 to 6 calls on one card from the next day's races and Block X targets. Every leg has to hit. Harder legs and longer cards pay more points. A staked game: stake at least 1 VHP in the Combo Card to play.

    How it works

    The legs. Win: the horse wins the race. Show: it finishes in the top 3. Top half: it finishes in the top half of the field. Block X: the producer signs that block (every 2,400th block). A horse must sign at least one block to count. More games add their own legs as they launch.

    What a leg is worth. Each leg is priced from its chance, using the Producer Race's latest handicap card when your card is sent:

    leg worth = 0.5 ÷ chance, from ×0.25 to ×3

    A leg keeps the worth it had when you sent the card. Only if the leg's chance has risen by more than 10% by the time it locks (for example because VHP moved to its producer) does it take the lower worth from the race card in force at the lock. Worths can drop, never rise, so a long shot can't keep its ×3 after its producer gets bigger.

    A leg that hits half the time is worth ×1. An easy leg, like a big producer finishing in the top half, is worth less than ×1; a hard one up to ×3. Race chances come from 4,000 simulated races on the card; Block X from each producer's share of VHP.

    The card. Points = the base for its number of legs × every leg's worth. There's no cap: if you can build it, you can win it.

    Fair rules. A race takes up to 3 legs, one of each type, on different horses; a block takes one. Each producer can be on your card only once, since one good day would carry all its legs together. If a race or block is void, that leg is dropped and the card scores on the rest (with at least 2 legs). A card is final when every leg is final; results come from irreversible blocks that match on two independent nodes.

    The week. Your best 10 cards count. The page and the recorder run the same scoring file, so the points shown are the points paid.
