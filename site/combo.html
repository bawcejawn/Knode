Combo Card

Build a card of 2 to 6 legs from the next day's races and Block X targets. If every leg hits, the card pays; the more legs and the harder they are, the more points. One miss and the card scores 0. Points only, never money: a staked game with a weekly leaderboard.

    A week is the sum of your best 10 cards. Ties go to more cards that hit, then the earlier last hit. Staked rules: the top 30% of staked players with at least 1 point share the Combo Card bucket. A week is final 12 hours after it ends, once every leg is final.

    How it works

    The legs. Win: the horse wins the race. Show: it finishes in the top 3. Top half: it finishes in the top half of the field. Block X: the producer signs that block (every 2,400th block). A horse must sign at least one block to count.

    What a leg is worth. Each leg is priced from its chance, using the Producer Race's latest handicap card when your card is sent:

    leg × = 0.5 ÷ chance, between ×0.25 and ×3

    A leg that hits half the time is worth ×1. An easy leg (a big producer in the top half) is worth less than ×1; a hard one up to ×3. Race chances come from 4,000 simulated races on the card; Block X from each producer's share of VHP.

    The card. Points = the base for its number of legs × every leg's worth, at most 150 points.

    Fair rules. No two legs on the same race or block, and a producer can be in only one race leg per card, since a producer's good day would carry them both. If a race or block is void, that leg is dropped and the card scores on the rest (with at least 2 legs). A card is final when every leg is final; results come from irreversible blocks that match on two independent nodes.

    The week. Your best 10 cards count. The page and the recorder run the same scoring file, so the points shown are the points paid.
