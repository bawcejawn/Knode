Times are "about": Koinos makes a block about every 3 seconds, so a target's time drifts a little. One pick per block; change it until the lock, up to 5 times. Up to 30 picks a day (changes count). Leaderboard

Ranked by points; ties go to more right picks, then the earlier last right pick. Two boards, as in every Knode Games game: Staked (wallets holding at least 1 VHP or staking in Knode Games) and Free play (everyone else, guests too). The weekly free-play prize, 2 VHP for 1st and 1 VHP for 2nd (ties share), goes to the top of the Free play board among wallets that picked on at least 3 different days that week. A week is final 12 hours after it ends.

"Points per 100 picks" is what always picking that producer would score on average. Every producer with at least a third of the top producer's VHP is equally good on average; the cap of ×3 keeps a lucky long shot from running away with the week.

How it works

The targets. Every block whose number is a multiple of 2,400 is a target, about every 2 hours. The next 12 are always open. Nobody chooses them.

The lock. The Picks contract refuses a pick once the chain reaches 100 blocks before the target, so every pick is in before anyone can know who signs it.

The score. Koinos picks who signs each block in proportion to VHP, so big producers sign more often. A right pick scores

min(3, top producer's VHP ÷ your producer's VHP)

with the VHP from the hourly handicap card at the lock (on the testnet the Block X recorder takes that card itself, since the testnet has no Producer Race). A wrong pick scores 0.

Testnet. Same contract code as mainnet except three settings (its owner and 10-minute waits instead of 48 hours), proven by the build script. Its data lives on its own branch (testnet-data), and its wallet never touches your mainnet wallets.
