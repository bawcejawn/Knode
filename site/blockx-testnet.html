How it works

The targets. Every block whose number is a multiple of 2,400 is a target, about every 2 hours. The next 12 are always open. Nobody chooses them.

The lock. The Picks contract refuses a pick once the chain reaches 100 blocks before the target, so every pick is in before anyone can know who signs it. You can change a pick up to 5 times, and make up to 30 picks a day (changes count).

The score. Koinos picks who signs each block in proportion to VHP, so big producers sign more often. A right pick scores

min(3, top producer's VHP ÷ your producer's VHP)

with the VHP from the hourly handicap card at the lock. A wrong pick scores 0. Ties on the board go to more right picks, then the earlier last right pick.

Prizes. Each week the top 2 of the Free play board (wallets that picked on at least 3 days) win 2 and 1 VHP on mainnet. Here on the testnet, prizes are listed but never paid.

Testnet. Same contract code as mainnet except three settings (its owner and 10-minute waits instead of 48 hours), proven by the build script. Your Test wallet lives only in this browser and never touches your mainnet wallets.
