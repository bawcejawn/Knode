The Knode Games app

Put Knode Games on your home screen

Knode Games installs straight from the website: no app store, nothing to download, always the latest version. It opens full-screen like any other app.

One tap

Your browser can install Knode Games right away.

Installed. Look for the Knode Games icon on your home screen or in your apps.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app when it appears, or open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install.

Computer

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a screen with a down arrow).
  3. Click Install. Knode Games opens in its own window.
