Producer Race

Every Koinos block producer is a horse. Each block it signs moves it forward, and blocks older than 24 hours drop off, so the race never ends. Smaller producers get a handicap, so every horse has the same chance of leading. Guess who leads next. It's free and just for fun.

        How the handicap keeps it fair

        Same speed. Koinos picks who signs each block in proportion to VHP, so a producer with a third of the leader's VHP signs about a third as many blocks. Moving it 3 steps per block gives every horse the same expected speed. The biggest producer always moves exactly 1 step per block.

        Same chance. Same speed isn't quite enough. A small producer's few blocks come in lucky and unlucky bursts, so over a day the streaky small horses would win most races. The handicap trims that with a "luck" term:

        handicap = (leader's VHP ÷ your VHP) × (1 − c ÷ √k) ÷ (1 − c ÷ √k of the leader)

        Here k is the blocks a producer is expected to sign in 24 hours, and luck in a count of k blocks is about √k. The page re-tunes c from today's field by running thousands of simulated 24-hour races (the same races for every visitor), picking the value where every horse's chance of leading is as close to equal as it gets.

        Handicaps follow each producer's current share of producing VHP, re-read every 10 minutes. When VHP moves, the handicaps move with it.
