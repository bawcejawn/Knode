Survivor

Each day, pick one Koinos producer you think stays out of the day's out-zone: the bottom of the handicapped block count. Two lives, each producer once a season. Outlast everyone. Seasons are two weeks. Free, with 4 VHP and 2 VHP prizes each season once the Knode Games vault is live.

Your picks

Picks open when the KnodeGames Picks contract is live and Season 1 is set. Until then you can watch today's live standings and the handicaps.

One pick per day, open 7 days ahead. A day locks at 23:55 UTC the night before, so every pick is in before any of its blocks. Change a pick until then, up to 5 times. Up to 20 picks a day (changes count). The Mana is paid by Knode's Mana sponsor.

Handicaps

Waiting for the recorder…

ProducerBlocks (7 days)A dayHandicap

Handicap = the top producer's blocks ÷ this producer's blocks, over the 7 days ending two days before. So every producer has the same expected score, and the handicap is known a full day before the lock.

How it works

A season. Two weeks, Monday to Monday (UTC). Pick for day 1 to join; after day 1 locks, nobody new can join until the next season.

Your daily pick. One producer a day. Its score is the blocks it signs that day times its handicap:

score = blocks today × (top producer's blocks ÷ its blocks, last 7 days)

The out-zone. The lowest scores of the day. It starts at a quarter of the field on day 1 and grows to half by day 14 (with 17 producers: 4 on day 1, 8 on day 14). A tie at the line is safe.

Lives. You have 2. You lose one if your producer lands in the out-zone, or if you have no valid pick that day (no pick, a producer not in the field, or one you already used). Each producer once a season. If nobody still in has a safe pick that day (each pick landed in the out-zone, or there was no valid pick), nobody loses a life for the out-zone, but the producer still counts as used. A missing pick, a producer not in the field, or one used before always costs a life. You can pick up to 7 days ahead, but each day's field is set the day before: check early picks once it's out.

The end. The season ends when 2 or fewer players are left, or after day 14. Ranking: who lasted longest, then lives left, then the total score of your picks.

Prizes. 4 VHP for 1st and 2 VHP for 2nd each season, paid from Knode's node rewards, in a season with at least 3 players who could win and made 2 or more valid picks. A player knocked out by a missed pick can't win a prize. Prizes start when the Knode Games vault is live; seasons played before that are for fun and nothing is owed for them. You need at least 1 VHP in your wallet (or at least 1 VHP staked in Knode) at every daily check while you're in, so empty wallets can't farm the prize.

Knode's own producers. Knode runs two producers (the Knode pool and Knode Swap nodes). They're in the field like any other, so you can pick them. If one of them has a bad day, it lands in the out-zone like anyone else's would.

Fair checks. Results come from irreversible blocks that match on two independent nodes, and the scoring code is public: the page and the recorder run the same file.
