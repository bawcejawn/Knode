How to play

  1. Draft five producersAny five from the price list, costing 100 or less. Prices follow each node's recent form.
  2. Name a captainYour captain scores double. Move the armband any day, as your one change.
  3. Save before the lockTeams lock 5 minutes before each day. Plan days ahead whenever you like.
  4. Score every day100 points for a producer that signs exactly its fair share of blocks, up to 130.
Full rules

Your team. Five different producers from the week's price list, within a budget of 100 (the salary cap). The first one is your captain and scores double. Save a team for the next day; it carries on until you change it.

A fresh start every week. New prices come out before each week, and Monday's team can be any team that fits them. Last week's team carries into Monday only if it still fits (all five on the list, within 100).

Then one change a day. From one day to the next you may swap one producer, or move the captain's armband. A new producer can take the armband of the one it replaces as part of that one swap. A team with more changes, over the budget, or with a producer not on the list is ignored, and yesterday's team plays.

Points. Koinos picks who signs each block in proportion to VHP, so every producer has a fair share of blocks. Each day a producer scores

100 × blocks it signed ÷ blocks it was expected to sign

(at most 130), with the expected share from the Producer Race's hourly VHP card. 100 means it signed exactly its share; a node that misses blocks scores less. Your week is the sum of your days; a day before you join scores 0.

Prices. The price list comes out Saturday 00:00 UTC (Friday evening in the Americas) for the next week, two days before it starts, and never changes after that. A producer's price follows its form over the 7 days before: 20 for the middle of the pool, up to 40 for the most reliable, down to 8 for the least. Its fingerprint (sha256) is published with it, so nobody can change it later.

Plan ahead. Once the prices are out you can line up your team, and a change for any later day of the week, all at once. A planned day can still be changed until its own lock, so planning early never ties your hands. If changing an earlier day would make a planned one break the one-change rule, the page tells you.

Locks. The Picks contract refuses a team 5 minutes before its day starts, so nobody can pick knowing the blocks. Picking late gives no edge on prices (they're fixed) or on VHP moves (each producer's expected share follows its VHP every hour); the only fresh news is a node's health, which the price list shows everyone (Yesterday and Today).

Prizes. Fantasy is a staked game: the Fantasy share of the Knode Games vault's weekly reward goes to the top 30% of Fantasy stakers with at least 1 point. A week is final 12 hours after it ends.

Weekly Rival. Challenge one player for the coming week. Whoever scores more that week wins. Your record counts the challenges you made and those you received. A match counts only if both of you hold a Knode Games stake and had a team on all 7 days, so nobody piles up wins against throwaway wallets.

All time. Every finished week adds your points to the all-time table: total, weeks played and best week. Bragging rights only; prizes are weekly.

Fair checks. Days are scored from irreversible blocks that match on two independent nodes. The page and the recorder run the same scoring file (fantasy-math.js).
