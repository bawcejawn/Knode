How Mana works

A wallet's tank holds as much Mana as it has KOIN: 100 KOIN means up to 100 Mana. VHP doesn't add Mana.

Each transaction uses a little (a game answer is about a third of one Mana). An empty tank refills completely in 5 days, at the same steady speed the whole way, so a wallet gets back a fifth of its KOIN every day.

Moving KOIN out of a wallet also needs Mana at least equal to the amount moved.
