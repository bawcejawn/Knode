Move your Knode Games guest wallet

Knode Games moved to knodegames.com on 28 October 2026. A guest wallet made on the old address (bawcejawn.github.io) is still kept in this browser at the old address. This page lets you take it with you, then erase it here. Nothing on this page goes over the internet.

Looking for an old guest wallet in this browser…

1. Save your guest key

Your guest key is like a password for your guest wallet. Anyone who has it can play as you, so keep it private.

2. Put it on knodegames.com

Open knodegames.com, choose Connect wallet → Play as a guest → Restore a guest key, and paste the key. Check that the address shown there matches your address.

3. Erase it from the old address

Only once it works on knodegames.com.
