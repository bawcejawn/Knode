Scouting report

NodePer hourFormHandicapLast 24 h

Form is what a node signed in the 48 hours before the field was set, against the VHP share each hour's race card gave it (kept between 0.80 and 1.20). The handicap already allows for it: a node that keeps missing blocks gets a bigger multiplier, and one that signs extra gets a smaller one, so no node starts with an edge you could look up. Last 24 h compares the last day's blocks with the node's VHP share × its form, what the handicap expects: 100% is on par. Who runs hot or cold on the day is the luck. Big nodes are steady; small nodes are boom or bust.

Leaderboard

Points: 1 for each cut your node survives, plus 5 for riding the last node standing without jumping, or 3 after a jump. Ranked by points, then rides, then fewer jumps; players equal on all three share the place. On the testnet nothing is paid.

How it works

Practice. Your picks go to the Koinos testnet with free test KOIN. The race is the real mainnet producers, read live: the testnet has only one producer, so it can't hold a race. Nothing here is paid and nothing counts on Knode Games.

Two timings. Fast test: a cut every 20 minutes, an event every 4 hours (00:00, 04:00, 08:00 … UTC); picks open 4 hours ahead and lock 10 minutes before the start. Real timing: the live game's timing, daily at 14:00 UTC with a cut every hour; picks open 24 hours ahead and lock at 13:50 UTC.

The field. Producers expected to sign at least 20 blocks an hour, from a Producer Race card taken before picks open.

The handicap. Big nodes sign more blocks, so smaller nodes move further per block. Each node's form (what it actually signed in the 48 hours before the field is set, against its VHP) is built in, and a small luck trim, set for each event, gives every node about the same chance to be last standing. The multipliers are fixed for the whole event.

The cut. At each cut, the node with the fewest steps since the start is knocked out. Ties go to fewer blocks, then less VHP.

Your run. Before the start, pick your node and, if you like, a backup. The first time your node is knocked out, your backup takes over automatically if it's still standing (one jump per event, and riding the last node after a jump scores +3 instead of +5). No backup, a backup that's already out, or losing the node you jumped to, and your run ends (your points so far count). Nothing can be picked or changed once the race starts.

Fair checks. Results come from irreversible blocks that match on two independent Koinos nodes, and the page and the recorder run the same rules file.
