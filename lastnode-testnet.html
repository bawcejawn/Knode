Scouting report

NodePer hourHandicapLast 24 h

"Last 24 h" is how many blocks a node actually signed compared with what its VHP says it should: 100% is on par. Nodes that miss blocks fall behind, and that's where the skill is. Big nodes are steady; small nodes are boom or bust.

Leaderboard

Points: 1 for each cut your node survives, plus 5 for riding the last node standing without jumping, or 3 after a jump. Ranked by points, then rides, then fewer jumps; players equal on all three share the place. On the testnet nothing is paid.

How it works

Practice. Your picks go to the Koinos testnet with free test KOIN. The race is the real mainnet producers, read live: the testnet has only one producer, so it can't hold a race. Nothing here is paid and nothing counts on Knode Games.

Two timings. Fast test: a cut every 20 minutes, an event every 4 hours (00:00, 04:00, 08:00 … UTC); your pick locks 15 minutes before the start; after your node is cut you can jump until 5 minutes past that cut. Real settings: the live game's timing, daily at 14:00 UTC, a cut every hour; your pick locks at 13:10 UTC; jump until 10 minutes past the cut.

The field. Producers expected to sign at least 20 blocks an hour, from a Producer Race card taken before picks open.

The handicap. Big nodes sign more blocks, so smaller nodes move further per block. A small luck trim, set for each event, gives every node about the same chance to be last standing.

The cut. At each cut, the node with the fewest steps since the start is knocked out. Ties go to fewer blocks, then less VHP.

Your run. Drop in on one node. If it's knocked out, jump once to a node still standing, or set a backup in advance and it jumps for you. Lose a second node, or miss the jump, and your run ends (your points so far count).

Fair checks. Results come from irreversible blocks that match on two independent Koinos nodes, and the page and the recorder run the same rules file.
